What is FactorGPT?

FactorGPT is an intelligent quantitative factor mining system that leverages LLM agents to automatically discover, evaluate, and select alpha factors from multi-source financial data. It combines the creative reasoning of Large Language Models with rigorous statistical validation to uncover novel predictive signals in A-share markets.
